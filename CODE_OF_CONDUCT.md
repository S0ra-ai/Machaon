# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that builds this project:

- being precise about what is verified and what is not;
- assuming good faith and asking before assuming;
- accepting that a claim needs evidence;
- giving credit for prior work, especially when we are building on someone
  else's experiment.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or personal attacks;
- publishing others' private information without permission;
- presenting unverified results as verified;
- using this project to remove safety behaviour from a deployed model.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer listed in `SECURITY.md`. Reports are handled
confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits, or contributors that
violate this code.
